Privacy Policy

Synapz Music — last updated 12 August 2026

Synapz Music is a music player for Android, Windows, macOS and the web. This policy covers all of them. It is written to be read, not to cover anyone legally at your expense.

What we collect

Nothing at all until you sign in. You can browse, search and play music without an account, and in that case no personal data leaves your device.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your email address, display name and profile picture
  • Playlists you create, songs you like, and your listening history
  • Listen Along rooms you host or join, while they are active

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, files, advertising identifiers, or any analytics about how you use the app. There is no advertising in Synapz Music, and no data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and protected by row-level security so only your signed-in account can read your rows. Playback happens directly between your device and the music source.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — authentication only, if you use it.
  • Supabase — stores your account, playlists and likes.
  • Audius — an open music network. Streaming a track sends a request to their servers.
  • YouTube — some tracks play through the official YouTube embedded player, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. These play with the video visible and stop when you leave the app.

What is kept on your device

Your sign-in session, liked songs, and a cached copy of the music catalogue are stored locally so the app starts quickly and works offline. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Deleting your data

Sign out to disconnect the device. To delete your account and everything attached to it, email zingua816@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and it will be removed within 30 days.

Children

Synapz Music is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it and the new version appears here.
